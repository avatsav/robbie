    The proposed rhythm
    80% everyday · 20% playful.
    Neutral rests: 6–12 seconds.
    Typing: neutral until 3 seconds quiet.
    Both hops share a 60-second cooldown.
    60 seconds idle → sleepy nods.
    90 seconds → tuck in and sleep.
    Asleep: slow breathing, no idle emotes.
    On return: stir → stand → blink → idle.
    Typing keeps the wake-up moving; submit skips straight to thinking.
    Each group uses a shuffled bag, avoiding immediate repeats. Busy and completion scenes take priority. Completion settles before the palette advances.
